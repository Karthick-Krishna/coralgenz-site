Security Model

Zero-Knowledge Architecture Pipeline

How your data travels safely from your local machine into encrypted distributed cloud storage.

End-to-End Cryptographic Flow

The master encryption key is derived locally and never sent over the network.

STEP 01

Key Derivation

Master password converted to 256-bit key via Argon2id with random 128-bit salt in browser.

STEP 02

Client-Side Cipher

File payload encrypted with AES-256-GCM generating unique IV and authentication tag.

STEP 03

Cipher Transmission

Encrypted ciphertext chunked and streamed over TLS 1.3 to geo-redundant storage nodes.

STEP 04

Local Decryption

Only authenticated users with the master key can decrypt bytes locally upon download.

Everything About Vault Cryptography & Security

Clear answers on encryption standards, zero-knowledge proofs, recovery protocols, and enterprise privacy.

Zero-Knowledge means that only you hold the keys to decrypt your information. Your master password is used locally by your browser or client application to derive the AES-256 decryption key. Coralgenz servers only receive and store unreadable, encrypted ciphertext. Neither our engineers nor any third party can access your raw files.
Because we do not store your master password or encryption keys, we cannot reset your password directly. However, during account setup, Coralgenz Vault generates a 24-word cryptographic Emergency Recovery Phrase that you can safely print or store in a physical location to regain access.
