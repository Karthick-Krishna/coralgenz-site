Coralgenz Vault Architecture

Coralgenz Vault is the flagship product embodying our proprietary .secure format. Engineered from the ground up to protect high-stakes enterprise intellectual property, legal filings, financial audit reports, and healthcare records, Coralgenz Vault enforces absolute zero-knowledge security across multi-cloud infrastructure.

Unlike standard cloud drives that decrypt files on virtual machines, Coralgenz Vault never possesses decryption keys, master passwords, or plaintext copies. Data is locked and unlocked strictly inside the user's browser sandbox, rendering server hypervisor breaches completely powerless.

LAYER 01
Client Sandbox

WebCrypto isolated execution context prevents browser extension snooping and OS hooks.

LAYER 02
Salt & Nonce Entropy

CSPRNG (Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator) generates nonces per file.

LAYER 03
AES-256-GCM Engine

Galois Counter Mode ensures authenticated encryption and automatic 128-bit GMAC integrity validation.

LAYER 04
Decentralized Export

Deliver files via email, USB, or cloud without worrying about third-party interception.

EXECUTIVE FAQ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions About Coralgenz Vault

What happens if an attacker breaches the Coralgenz Vault database?
Even if an attacker gains complete access to our database, they obtain only scrambled, high-entropy ciphertexts. Without your personal master key—which exists solely in your browser memory—the stolen data is mathematically impossible to decrypt.
Can our enterprise deploy Coralgenz Vault on private company servers?
Yes. We offer self-hosted enterprise deployments compatible with Docker and Kubernetes for on-premises air-gapped data centers, Microsoft Azure, AWS, and private clouds.
Does SecureVault support role-based team file sharing?
Yes. SecureVault supports asymmetric public-key cryptography (RSA-OAEP / ECDH) allowing employees to share encrypted vaults with specific colleagues without revealing passwords.
