SIMPLE ANSWERS FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Our Partner Services

What does being a Salesforce ISV Partner mean?
It means we are an official Salesforce Independent Software Vendor (ISV) Partner to build commercial AppExchange products, Second-Generation Managed Packages (2GP), and custom Lightning Web Components that pass strict security and quality checks.
What does Microsoft Developer Partner & Collaborate Member mean?
As a Microsoft Developer Partner and active member of Microsoft Collaborate and the Microsoft Edge developer ecosystem, our team has direct access to Microsoft engineering resources, early preview tools, and Azure cloud infrastructure to build reliable cloud apps for Windows, Edge, and Azure.
What does being an Official Shopify Partner mean?
As an official Shopify Partner, we specialize in high-speed headless storefronts, custom Shopify app development, Storefront GraphQL API integrations, and Liquid theme modernizations that deliver sub-second page loads and higher checkout conversion rates.
Can you connect our Shopify store with our Salesforce CRM and Azure cloud?
Yes, absolutely. We build direct, real-time sync bridges that automatically send orders and customer inquiries from Shopify directly into your Salesforce CRM and back them up securely in your Microsoft Azure database with zero manual effort.
What if we only need help with one platform (e.g. only Shopify or only Salesforce)?
We frequently take on standalone projects! Whether you only need a custom Shopify store, an AppExchange package for Salesforce, or an Azure AI integration, our team handles each scope with complete dedication and rapid turnaround times.
How do we get started with Coralgenz?
Simply email us at contact@coralgenz.co.in or submit an inquiry through our contact page. You will receive an initial response within 24 hours directly from our core founders to discuss your project requirements, scope, and timeline.
