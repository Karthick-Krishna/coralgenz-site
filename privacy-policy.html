Privacy Policy & Corporate Policies

At Coralgenz Global, privacy and confidentiality are core engineering disciplines. This policy clearly outlines how we protect your information, honor intellectual property, and safeguard client communications.

Effective: October 2026 • Version: 2.4 • Jurisdiction: Coimbatore, India

1. Our Privacy Commitment in Plain English

Coralgenz Global ("Coralgenz", "we", "our", or "us") operates as an enterprise software development, IT engineering, and product company headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

We hold a simple, steadfast philosophy: your data belongs to you. We do not track you across the web, we do not monetize user details, and we treat every codebase, architecture blueprint, or inquiry with highest-grade confidentiality.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Collected

We only collect information that you explicitly and voluntarily provide to us through our direct touchpoints:

  • Contact Inquiries: Name, professional email address, company name, and project scope when submitting inquiries via email or our contact portal.
  • Partner Intake Applications: Company name, technical domain, contact person, and engineering requirements submitted through our official partner intake portal.
  • Standard Server Logs: Standard HTTP request data (anonymized IP address, user agent, requested URL) collected strictly for security telemetry, DDoS defense, and uptime verification.
What We Do NOT Collect: We never collect payment card numbers on this website, sensitive personal identifiers, background biometric telemetry, or persistent tracking cookies.

3. Confidentiality, NDA & Client Code Protection

As software engineers working with innovative startups and enterprise systems (including Salesforce ISV, Microsoft Ecosystem, and custom distributed software), confidentiality is paramount:

  • Mutual NDA Governance: All confidential project disclosures, system architecture specs, and proprietary workflows can be protected under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to engagement.
  • Restricted Access: Client codebases, API credentials, and internal schemas are restricted strictly to authorized engineering personnel working directly on the project.
  • Zero Repurposing: Client proprietary algorithms, data models, or specialized intellectual property are never reused or shared with any other client or external entity.

4. Absolute Zero Data Monetization

Coralgenz Global does not sell, lease, trade, license, or barter your personal information, corporate details, or technical documentation to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing conglomerates. We generate revenue strictly through software development, IT services, and proprietary technology products.

5. Security & Encryption Architecture

We engineer our public and private infrastructure following modern security best practices:

  • HTTPS & TLS 1.3: All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted with modern TLS ciphers.
  • Zero-Knowledge Security Research: Our innovations (such as the Coralgenz Vault engine and the proprietary .secure cryptographic format) embody our commitment to cryptographic data privacy.
  • Hardened Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud deployments use isolated networks, strict IAM least-privilege policies, and regular dependency vulnerability audits.

6. Cookies & Website Telemetry

Our website is designed for speed, security, and respect for user autonomy:

  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not load invasive remarketing pixels or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Essential Session Storage: We may use minimal, functional browser storage (such as localStorage) solely to preserve user preferences (e.g., interface theme or menu states).

7. Regulatory Alignment & Your Rights

We operate in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and respect international privacy principles (including the European Union GDPR standards):

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request a summary of any contact information we hold regarding your inquiry.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): You may request the immediate deletion of your contact submissions or partner intake data at any time.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: You may opt out of any direct email communication at any time by simply replying with your preference.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our leadership desk at contact@coralgenz.co.in. Requests are acknowledged and processed within 48 business hours.
