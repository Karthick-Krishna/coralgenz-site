01

Who We Are & Scope of this Policy

💡
In Simple English:

This privacy policy explains how Coralgenz Global handles your personal details when you visit our website, ask for software services, or work with us. We are based in Coimbatore, India, and we treat your privacy with total honesty and zero gimmicks.

Coralgenz Global is an enterprise software engineering company headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. This Corporate Privacy Policy applies to all services, web portals, technical consultations, and software development engagements provided under the Coralgenz Global brand.

Under Section 2(i) of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), Coralgenz Global acts as a Data Fiduciary determining why and how personal data is processed. Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we serve as the Data Controller for our website visitors and business clients.

Note on Coralgenz Vault: If you are using our encrypted container product Coralgenz Vault or files in the .secure format (invented and introduced by Coralgenz Global, and all rights are owned by the company), those are governed by a separate, dedicated Vault Zero-Knowledge Policy because files are encrypted inside your browser before reaching any server.

03

Why We Process Your Data (Lawful Grounds)

💡
In Simple English:

Under the law, a company must have a clear, valid reason to process your information. We process data only because you asked us to help with a project, because we have a signed contract, or to obey the law.

Under Section 4 of India’s DPDP Act 2023 and Article 6 of GDPR, we process personal information exclusively under these legal grounds:

  • With Your Direct Consent: When you voluntarily fill out our contact form or message us for consultation. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • To Fulfill a Contract: When we build custom applications, refactor code, deploy cloud systems, or deliver technical support under a commercial agreement.
  • To Obey Statutory Laws: Maintaining business accounts, financial records, and tax filings required by the Government of India.
  • For Legitimate Security Defense: Keeping our servers safe from automated spam bots, DDoS attacks, and unauthorized intrusion.
04

Your Code, Intellectual Property & Mutual NDA

💡
In Simple English:

Your source code belongs 100% to you. Before you share a single line of proprietary code with us, we sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). We will NEVER train public AI models on your code or share it with anyone else.

As an enterprise engineering partner, we treat client source code, database architectures, and business logic with the highest level of confidentiality:

  • Mutual NDA by Default: We execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to reviewing client repositories or internal technical specifications.
  • Zero Public AI Training: Your codebases are never fed into third-party public AI training models or public code generators.
  • Isolated Workstations: Our engineers develop on encrypted hardware with full disk encryption (FileVault / BitLocker) and hardware multi-factor authentication.
  • Total IP Ownership: Upon settlement of agreed project milestones, all source code, git histories, documentation, and deployment keys are transferred 100% to the client.

06

How Long We Keep Your Data & Safe Deletion

💡
In Simple English:

We do not hold onto your data forever. Inquiries are kept for up to 2 years, tax invoices for 7 years (as required by tax laws), and temporary server logs are erased every 90 days.

  • Project Inquiries & Technical Discovery: Kept for 24 months from our last conversation, after which inactive contact records are purged.
  • Invoices & Tax Accounting: Kept for 7 years to comply with statutory commercial audits under Indian law.
  • Server Security Logs: Automatically rotated and permanently wiped every 90 days.
  • Client Staging Environments: Completely scrubbed and destroyed within 30 days of project sign-off and warranty completion.
07

Your Legal Rights (DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, CCPA)

💡
In Simple English:

You have full control over your personal details. You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to fix mistakes, or ask us to delete it completely. We answer all requests for free within 30 days.

Right to Access

Ask for a free copy of all personal details we hold about you, and see who has handled it.

Right to Correct & Update

Ask us to update outdated phone numbers, email addresses, or incorrect company details immediately.

Right to Erasure (Delete Me)

Ask us to permanently wipe your personal details from our systems when we no longer need them.

Right to Nominate (DPDP Sec. 14)

Under Indian law, choose a trusted representative to handle your data rights if you are unable to.

How to exercise your rights: Email us directly at grievance@coralgenz.co.in or contact@coralgenz.co.in. We acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and complete your request within 30 days without any fee.

08

How We Keep Your Information Safe

💡
In Simple English:

We use military-strength encryption (TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest). Only authorized senior engineers can access our infrastructure, protected by two-factor authentication.

  • Encrypted Connections: All web traffic is locked with modern TLS 1.3 encryption and HSTS (HTTP Strict Transport Security).
  • Encrypted Storage: Database backups and cloud disks are encrypted with hardware-accelerated AES-256.
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): All developer and management accounts require physical security keys or hardware 2FA tokens.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Real-time automated firewall monitoring and vulnerability scanning against zero-day exploits.

10

Cookies & Local Storage

💡
In Simple English:

We do NOT use creepy advertising cookies or Facebook/Google retargeting pixels. We only store essential technical settings (like dark mode and security tokens) inside your browser.

  • Essential Functionality: Small technical tokens used to remember your theme preferences and protect forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: No Meta Pixel, no Google AdSense, no invasive tracking cookies.
  • Privacy-Preserving Speed Metrics: Aggregate page load statistics are completely anonymous and never linked to your personal identity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell my email address to marketing companies?
No. Never. Coralgenz Global operates on a strict zero-monetization policy for personal information. We make our revenue by building world-class enterprise software, not by selling our users' data.
How is this policy different from the Coralgenz Vault policy?
This Corporate Policy covers our company website, software consulting, and business interactions. Coralgenz Vault is an isolated Zero-Knowledge product using the .secure format (invented and introduced by Coralgenz Global, and all rights are owned by the company) where files are mathematically encrypted client-side in browser memory with AES-256-GCM. Learn more on our dedicated Vault Zero-Knowledge Policy.
How quickly will your team respond to my privacy inquiry?
We guarantee an initial acknowledgment within 24 to 48 business hours. All data access, correction, or deletion requests are resolved within 30 statutory days without any processing fee.
