FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Inquiries: Clear, straightforward answers about our engineering process, code ownership, sprint timelines, and pricing model.

01 What core services and solutions does Coralgenz deliver?
We provide full-lifecycle custom software engineering, modern web and mobile applications, high-scale cloud backend architecture, and enterprise API integrations. As official partners with Microsoft, Salesforce, and Shopify, we build resilient, high-speed digital products designed for high scale from day one.
02 Do we retain 100% intellectual property and source code ownership?
Yes, unconditionally. All source code, Git repositories, deployment configurations, architecture documentation, and visual assets belong 100% to your organization from the start. We do not use proprietary vendor lock-in mechanisms, licensing royalties, or restricted dependencies.
03 How do your development sprints and client communications operate?
We operate in focused weekly sprints. Every week, your team receives working software demos, live preview environments, and direct access to the core developers building your product. There are no middlemen, account managers, or bureaucratic delays.
04 How is project pricing structured? Are there any hidden costs?
Our pricing is transparent and milestone-based. Before kickoff, we define exact deliverable milestones and fixed costs for each phase. You only pay as agreed deliverables are completed and verified, with zero unexpected charges, surprise invoices, or hidden fees.
05 How does Coralgenz maintain application security and code quality?
We enforce strict clean-architecture guidelines, automated CI/CD security scanning, peer review protocols, and thorough multi-device testing. Every release is tested against modern performance benchmarks and encrypted with standard security best practices.
06 How fast can we kick off a new project with your team?
Once you submit your project requirements through our contact form or email, our engineering founders review your specifications and schedule a discovery call within 24 hours. We typically deliver a full architecture proposal, milestone breakdown, and start sprint 1 within days.
07 Where is Coralgenz Global located and what regions do you serve?
Coralgenz Global is headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, serving enterprise clients, fast-growing startups, and scale-ups worldwide across the Americas, Europe, Middle East, and Asia-Pacific with high-availability distributed agile delivery.
08 What proprietary technology has Coralgenz Global invented?
Coralgenz Global invented the world's first .secure browser-native zero-knowledge encrypted container format (featuring a 16-layer security architecture across 4 distinct tiers), the Coralgenz Vault workstation, Coralgenz Dynamic QR telemetry engine, and Coralgenz Cloud Compiler IDE.
09 Who are Coralgenz Global official technology partners?
Coralgenz Global is an official partner with Microsoft (Azure cloud solutions & DevOps), Salesforce (AppExchange ISV partner for 2GP & LWC), and Shopify (headless commerce & custom apps), backed by active co-engineering startup partnerships with Zevionix Technologies and Zorquelz Technologies.
10 How can clients get in touch with Coralgenz Global leadership?
Clients can reach our executive engineering desk directly via contact@coralgenz.net or support@coralgenz.net with a guaranteed 24-hour response SLA.
