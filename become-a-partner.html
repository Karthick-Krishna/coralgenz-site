APPLICATION FAQS

Who is eligible to apply for the Coralgenz Partner Program?

Our partnership program is exclusively open to early to growth-stage startups and mid-range companies (SaaS builders, scale-ups, and emerging digital enterprises). We dedicate our senior engineering leadership specifically to these organizations to ensure rapid agile delivery, zero bureaucratic overhead, and maximum engineering velocity.

Are international startups and mid-range companies eligible?

Yes. We partner with technology startups and mid-range companies globally across the United States, Europe, APAC, and India under bilateral non-disclosure agreements.

How does the partnership model work for eligible companies?

Eligible startups and mid-range companies collaborate with our core leadership on direct product development, scalable SaaS engineering, or integrated team pairing—providing deep architectural guidance and sprint execution without unnecessary friction.
