ARCH 01

What is Zero-Knowledge Architecture?

💡
In Simple English:

In ordinary cloud services (like Google Drive or Dropbox), the company holds the master key to your files. In Coralgenz Vault, your computer or phone locks the file using AES-256 encryption BEFORE uploading. Our servers only receive scrambled digital gibberish. We physically cannot read what is inside.

When Coralgenz Global invented the .secure format in Coimbatore, India, we eliminated the need to trust server administrators. The encryption and decryption happen exclusively in your browser’s volatile memory using the W3C Web Cryptography API.

// Non-custodial mathematical proof:
Server_Receives = Encrypted_Ciphertext_Blob
Server_Keys = NONE (0 keys stored or transmitted)
Server_Ability_To_Decrypt = MATHEMATICALLY IMPOSSIBLE

Even if an attacker gains physical access to our database disks, all they get is random encrypted data that cannot be decrypted without your password.

ARCH 02

Why 2,000,000 PBKDF2 Iterations Matter

💡
In Simple English:

Hackers use supercomputers to guess millions of passwords every second. By forcing your device to run 2,000,000 rounds of cryptographic math for each guess, guessing a password takes centuries instead of seconds.

Coralgenz Vault uses a military-grade key derivation pipeline inside your browser:

STEP 1 Password In Client RAM
STEP 2 HMAC-SHA-512 Dynamic Pepper
STEP 3 2M PBKDF2 Unique Salt
STEP 4 AES-256-GCM 256-Bit Cipher
STEP 5 SHA-256 Check Integrity Match
  • Dynamic Pepper + Salt: Protects against rainbow tables and pre-computed password cracking dictionaries.
  • AES-256-GCM Authenticated Encryption: If even a single byte of your file is altered in transit, the decryption immediately aborts with a tamper alert.
  • SHA-256 Integrity Verification: Confirms that the decrypted file matches the original binary byte-for-byte.

ARCH 04

RAM Scrubbing (zeroizeMemory Protocol)

💡
In Simple English:

Other websites leave opened files sitting in your computer's memory even after you switch tabs. Coralgenz Vault immediately overwrites memory with zeroes (.fill(0)) as soon as you close the file, so spyware cannot read it.

Our automated memory zeroization protocol wipes unencrypted bytes from browser RAM:

  • Immediate Overwrite: The unencrypted byte buffer is immediately overwritten with binary zeros (.fill(0)) before garbage collection triggers.
  • Automated Triggers: Executes when you lock the vault, close the browser tab, minimize the window, or when an idle timer expires.
  • Screen Guard Protection: If you switch away from the window or press screenshot keys, the container view is instantly blurred to prevent visual shoulder-surfing.
ARCH 05

Court Subpoenas & Government Requests

💡
In Simple English:

If the police or a court orders us to turn over your files, we literally cannot decrypt them. We do not have a master key or backdoor. All we can give them is the scrambled encrypted blob.

The Mathematics of Subpoena Compliance

Coralgenz Global obeys all lawful court orders and search warrants. However, because we operate under a true Zero-Knowledge model, we do not possess the keys to decrypt your files. There is no administrative master override, no escrow key, and no secret backdoor.

ARCH 06

Your Password: No Reset, No Backdoor

💡
In Simple English:

Because we never save your password, we CANNOT reset your password if you forget it. Please write it down or keep it in a trusted password manager.

  • No "Forgot Password" Button: Real zero-knowledge encryption means nobody—not even our CEO or Chief Technology Officer—can reset your password.
  • Offline Decryption: Standalone .secure files can be decrypted completely offline without an internet connection, anywhere in the world.
  • Back Up Your Password: If you lose your password, your files will remain permanently encrypted ciphertext.

We do not scan your documents, train AI models on your files, or sell usage data. Your files are private to you and you alone.
