Executive Biography & Vision

Karthick Krishna M. S. is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Coralgenz Global, an unfunded Indian IT & product engineering house headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Known for his hands-on executive leadership, he integrates high-level corporate governance with deep technical architecture and lead UI/UX design.

He pioneered the Dual-Role Leadership Model at Coralgenz Global—ensuring every client engagement receives direct founder-level engineering oversight without bureaucratic middle management. Under his stewardship, Coralgenz Global expanded into an official partner of Microsoft and Salesforce while building an ecosystem of proprietary developer software serving over 50,000+ people worldwide.
